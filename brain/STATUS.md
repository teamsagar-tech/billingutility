# Digital Bills & Invoice System (RetailNode Digital Bills)

## Status: Operational / Staging
- **Port**: 3018 (assigned)
- **Tech Stack**: Vite + React, Tailwind CSS, PDF generation engine
- **Purpose**: Generates interactive digital receipts, tax invoices, and WhatsApp-friendly bills for apparel stores, replacing thermal paper printing by 100%.
- **Key Modules**:
  - `digital-invoice-system/`: Core invoice templates, barcode rendering, itemized discounts, GST breakdown.
  - `src/`: Interactive customer bill view, rating/feedback collector, store coupons, and loyalty points summary.
