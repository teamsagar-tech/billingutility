## 3. Detailed UI Element Breakdown

### A. Top Pre-Bill Engagement (Highest Eye-Level Visibility)
1. **Google Review Rating Prompt**:
   - Clean card: *"Rate Your Experience with us"*
   - 5 large interactive star outlines.
   - Official colorful Google `G` logo on the right.
2. **Facebook Follow Banner**:
   - Clean white card featuring the official Facebook logo and bold text *"Follow us on facebook"*.
3. **Quick Utility Bar**:
   - `Download PDF` and `Share Bill` quick actions.

### B. Loyalty Points Bar
- High-contrast emerald banner:
  - Left: `LOYALTY POINTS EARNED +18 pts`
  - Right: `TOTAL BALANCE 60 pts`

### C. Realistic Thermal Paper Receipt
- Floating white card with soft drop-shadow mimicking printed receipt paper.
- Store details: Store name, address, Bill #, date, customer mobile, payment mode (`UPI`).
- Itemized tabular breakdown: Product name, quantity, unit price, total line item amount.
- Tax computation summary (`GST Tax Included`).
- High-visibility **Grand Total** in bold green typography.

### D. Post-Bill Engagement & Omnichannel Retailing
- **Website CTA**: *"Visit our website for more information"* with a prominent royal blue `Shop Online ↗` button.
- **Dynamic Campaign Banner**: Dedicated visual advertisement space (e.g. *Durga Puja Offer - 50% OFF*).
- **Social Connect**: Instagram and WhatsApp circular icon badges.
- **Omnichannel Ordering Bar**: Sticky or prominent bar offering:
  - `🛍️ Shop Online`
  - `💬 WhatsApp Order`
- **Customer Purchase History Drawer**:
  - Displays customer's previous bills (e.g. `#1 Invoice #OOE7OJKM`, `#2 Invoice #N5SCV1DK`) allowing shoppers to look up past purchases anytime.
